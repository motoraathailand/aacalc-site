Currency converter that works offline

Travelling, shopping online or on a bad connection? AA Calc keeps the last rates it saved, plus a built-in table, and shows how old they are.

Questions

Is AA Calc free?

Yes. The app is free. The test version has no ads at all, and the version released to everyone shows small banner ads only (never on the calculator keypad).

Does it really work offline?

Yes. The calculator, the unit converter and the currency converter all work with no connection. Fonts, icons and a starter table of exchange rates are inside the app.

How are exchange rates updated offline?

They are not, and the app tells you so. Offline, it uses the last rates it saved and shows their date, or its built-in starter table if it has never been online. When you are online and the saved rates are older than 6 hours, it downloads fresh ones by itself. Rates are for reference only: banks and exchange booths use their own.

Is it private? Do I need to log in?

There is no login and no account. Your calculations and history stay on your phone. The only thing the app sends is a plain request for exchange rates (open.er-api.com) with no personal data, and it has no analytics or tracking tools. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I join the test?

Tap “Get the app” and follow the 5 steps on the join page: join the Google Group, use the same Google account as your phone, open the Play test link, install from Google Play, then keep the app for 14 days.

Why a test instead of a normal download?

AA Calc is in a Google Play closed test before it opens to everyone. Google asks for at least 12 testers to stay opted in for 14 days. Testers help us check the app on many phones, and the app will be on Google Play for everyone after that.

Which phones and countries?

Android phones and tablets with Android 7 or newer and Google Play, in every country.

Which languages?

Thai, English, Korean, Chinese and Hindi. AA Calc follows your phone language and falls back to English. The globe button is on every screen.
