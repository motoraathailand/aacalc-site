A calculator that converts units and currency. Even offline.

AA Calc looks and feels like the Samsung calculator, then adds a unit converter and a currency converter. No login, no sign-up. Open it and it is right back where you left off.

Small app. Three jobs done well.

  • Samsung-style calculator

    Big, calm keys in the One UI style, with history. Exact decimals (0.1 + 0.2 = 0.3) and a percent key that works like the Samsung one.

  • Unit converter

    Length, area, weight, volume, temperature and speed. Thai units are in too: rai, ngan, square wa and baht (gold weight).

  • Currency converter

    24 popular currencies, including THB, USD, KRW, EUR, JPY, CNY and INR. Shows when the rate was last updated.

  • Works fully offline

    Fonts, icons and a rates snapshot are inside the app. With no signal it uses your last saved rates and says so on screen.

  • Opens where you left off

    Used the currency converter last? That is what you see next time, with your last unit category and currency pair.

  • 5 languages, light and dark

    Thai, English, Korean, Chinese and Hindi. It follows your phone language, falls back to English, and the globe button is always in reach.

Simple and private

  • No login and no account. Nothing to sign up for.
  • Your calculations and history stay on your phone.
  • The only thing sent is a plain request for exchange rates (open.er-api.com). It contains no personal data.
  • The closed-test version has no ads at all. The released app shows small banner ads only.
