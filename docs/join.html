Questions

Which Google account?

Use the same Gmail for all 3 steps (join the group, become a tester, install). It must be the account you use in Google Play on your Android device. With a different account you will not count as a tester.

Which devices work?

Android phones and tablets with Android 7 or newer.

How long do I keep the app?

Keep it installed for 14 days and use it as you normally would. Please don’t delete it in that time.

Do I need internet or an account?

No account and no login. Everything works offline. Only the exchange rates are refreshed when you are online (the app ships with a rates snapshot).

Does the test version have ads?

No. The closed-test version has no ads at all.

Where can I test it?

In every country. The test is open to anyone with a Google Play account.
